What this can and can't catch

Accepted: photos from the identifier's own Take photo button, and uploads that still carry their camera details. Cropping or editing in the phone's gallery usually keeps them.

  • Blocked: images with AI labels (Content Credentials or IPTC "made by AI" from ChatGPT / DALL·E, Adobe Firefly, Microsoft Designer, Google and others; Stable Diffusion, ComfyUI, InvokeAI or NovelAI settings; generator names such as Midjourney).
  • Blocked: uploads with no camera details. This catches AI images that were cropped, edited or screenshotted to remove their labels, but also real photos that lost their details, e.g. ones forwarded through WhatsApp or Messenger, downloaded from the web, or screenshots.
  • Still possible: someone who adds fake camera details with special software. That takes real effort, unlike cropping.
  • For now this stricter rule is only on this test page; the identifier pages still block only AI-labelled photos.
  • AI check judges the picture itself in your browser (it downloads 172 MB the first time), so it also works on screenshots and cropped images. The private reports page runs it on every report and marks each one "AI-made" or "Real".
